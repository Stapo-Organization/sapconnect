05 Frequently asked
Who can buy at the Al Suwaidi cash & carry?

The Al Suwaidi depot serves the trade — pet shops, veterinary clinics, supermarkets, online retailers and other businesses. Walk-in trade and retail buyers are welcome.

Do I need an account to order?

No account is needed to visit and buy at the counter. For wholesale pricing and to order online for collection, create a free Muntajat business account — approval usually takes about one business day.

What are the opening hours in Al Suwaidi?

The depot is open Saturday to Thursday, 8:00am–11:00pm, and Friday 2:00pm–11:00pm. (Hours shown are indicative and confirmed by the branch.)

How many products are in stock?

Around 1,056 of our 1,208 demo catalogue products are in stock at Al Suwaidi right now, across 38 exclusive brands — from cat and dog food to grooming, bird, small-pet and aquarium supplies.

What payment methods are accepted?

The depot accepts cash and card. Business accounts can also arrange trade terms with their Muntajat account manager.
