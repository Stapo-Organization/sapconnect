01About the brand

Beaphar is a family-owned Dutch company that has cared for pets since 1942, when its founder began crafting gentle remedies for dogs and cats from the Netherlands. More than eighty years later, Beaphar has grown into one of the most trusted names in pet healthcare, with products reaching families in over 86 countries. The brand is built on a simple belief that every pet owner deserves easy access to quality care and affordable medicines, guided by four cornerstones: family, accessibility, quality, and care. From anti-parasite treatments and everyday health supplements to shampoos, grooming sprays, dental care, and nutrition, Beaphar serves dogs, cats, small pets, and birds with formulations developed and produced under strictly controlled standards.

In Saudi Arabia and across the GCC, Beaphar is brought to market by Muntajat, the brand's trusted and authorized distributor since 2004. As the Kingdom's leading B2B pet-products distributor, Muntajat keeps Beaphar's most relied-upon ranges, including Dog Health, Cat Health, and Dog Grooming, in consistent supply for retailers, clinics, and pet specialists nationwide. Every line is available at Muntajat's 10 cash-and-carry depots spread across the Kingdom, giving trade partners dependable stock, fair value, and the confidence that the Beaphar products they receive are genuine and ready for Saudi shelves.
