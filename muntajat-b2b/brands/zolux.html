01About the brand

Zolux is a French family-owned brand whose story began in 1933 as a humble seed shop on France's western coast, founded by Madeleine and Fernand Haurit. Over more than ninety years and four generations, that small store grew into one of Europe's most respected pet specialists — a creator, manufacturer, and distributor of habitats, accessories, and care essentials for dogs, birds, fish, and small pets. Guided by a simple philosophy, "do things better," Zolux designs thoughtful, durable products that put animal well-being first, from its spacious NEOLIFE small-pet cages and interconnecting Panas Colour igloos to its carefully engineered aquarium ranges. The result is a catalogue that pairs genuine French design with everyday practicality, trusted in pet shops across more than fifty countries.

In Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC, Muntajat is the trusted distributor that brings Zolux to local retailers. Since 2005, Muntajat has built its reputation as the Kingdom's leading B2B pet-products distributor, and it is proud to represent Zolux among its 38 exclusive brands. Saudi pet shops can source Zolux small-pet cages and igloos, small-pet supplies, fish aquariums and accessories, and bird and dog essentials directly from any of Muntajat's 10 cash-and-carry depots across the Kingdom — with the reliable stock, fair trade terms, and on-the-ground support that keep shelves full and customers loyal.
