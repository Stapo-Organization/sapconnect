01About the brand

Feline GO is a dedicated cat-and-dog lifestyle brand built around one simple idea: every pet deserves a comfortable, well-made place of its own. Designed for the way cats and dogs actually live, the range covers cosy cat beds and multi-level cat trees, sturdy scratchers that protect furniture and satisfy natural instincts, engaging cat toys, and snug dog beds and houses. With plush, supportive foam construction, practical sizing and a clean, home-friendly look, Feline GO products are made to be lived in, day after day — and the brand's companion Canine GO line extends the same comfort-first philosophy to dogs. It is a brand that understands the region's pet owners and the homes they share with their animals.

Across Saudi Arabia and the wider GCC, Muntajat is proud to be the trusted, authorized distributor of Feline GO. Since 2005, Muntajat has grown into the Kingdom's leading B2B pet-products distributor — 38 exclusive brands, around 4,452 active SKUs, and a nationwide logistics backbone built for retailers. Feline GO's cat beds and trees, dog beds and houses, and cat toys and scratchers are stocked and ready to order across all 10 of our cash-and-carry depots, giving pet shops, vets and e-commerce sellers dependable supply, fast fulfilment and a partner that knows this market. This dedicated brand page is our thank-you to Feline GO for trusting Muntajat to carry their name across the Kingdom.
