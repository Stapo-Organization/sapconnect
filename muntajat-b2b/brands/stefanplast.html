01About the brand

Founded in 1964 by Antonio Stefani in Villaganzerla di Castegnero, in Italy's Veneto region, Stefanplast has spent six decades perfecting the art of durable, beautifully engineered plastic products. Its 100% Made in Italy pet range — known the world over through the Peter Pet collection — pairs functional design with everyday practicality, from the iconic IATA-approved Gulliver travel carriers and the elegant matelassé Chic Collection to pet beds, feeding bowls, airtight food containers, cat toilets, and bird cages with accessories. Exported to more than seventy countries and produced at a scale of tens of millions of items each year, Stefanplast is trusted by pet owners and professionals alike for products that are safe, long-lasting, and unmistakably Italian in style.

In Saudi Arabia and across the wider GCC, Muntajat is the trusted, authorized distributor of Stefanplast. Since 2005 we have built our reputation as the Kingdom's leading B2B pet-products distributor — 38 exclusive brands, roughly 4,452 active SKUs, and a logistics network that keeps shelves stocked from Riyadh to Jeddah to the Eastern Province. The complete Stefanplast range for cats, dogs, and birds is available across all 10 of our cash-and-carry depots, giving retailers, clinics, and pet shops dependable wholesale access to authentic Italian quality — backed by Muntajat's reliable supply and service.
