01About the brand

Founded in 1977 in Creazzo, near Vicenza in northern Italy, MPS grew from a specialist in plastics and precision injection moulding into one of the most respected names in the European pet world. The family-run company controls every step in-house, from product study and mould-making through to moulding and finishing, and that craftsmanship shows in a catalogue built on durable, food-safe materials, intelligent ventilation, secure locks, and the clean, contemporary look that defines Made in Italy. Today MPS designs trusted homes and habitats for the animals families love most: roomy cages and cosy igloos for small pets, airy cages and accessories for birds, easy-to-keep aquariums for fish, and practical carriers and comforts for dogs.

In Saudi Arabia and across the GCC, MPS is distributed by Muntajat, the brand's trusted and authorized partner in the Kingdom. Since 2005, Muntajat has built the region's leading B2B pet-products network, with more than 75 international brands and around 4,452 active SKUs served through 10 cash-and-carry depots nationwide. For retailers and pet professionals, that means dependable access to genuine MPS small-pet cages and igloos, bird cages and accessories, and fish aquariums and supplies, backed by Muntajat's logistics, in-stock depth, and local expertise. This dedicated brand home is our thank-you to MPS for trusting Muntajat to bring authentic Italian quality to Saudi homes.
